STRONG / PROMISING / WEAK → **PROMISING**

**1. Three-second read: fails its own promise.** A caliper is not a calibration card. The tag *is* the metaphor, and the concept draws it as a square-cornered `<dl>` with `1px solid var(--card-border)` — visually identical to every other card on the page. It has no tag silhouette: no clipped corner, no reinforced eyelet, no string. "Nothing else round" and "square corners" actively forbid the two cues that make a shop-floor tag recognisable at image speed. So the left column reads "engineering drawing" and the right reads "spec table" — two familiar things, neither of them a tag. `threeSecondRead[3]` ("no percentage and no fill anywhere") is not perceivable in three seconds; absence requires comparison to memory.

**2. Craft: asymmetric.** Genuinely grade 10: micro ticks *only* in the 150–250 ms read window; the `--ink-900` line at y=97 seating the scale in the surface; butt-capped tolerance serifs; the empty `DUE` box, which is the best idea here. Grade 6: 9px mono `LC 0.5 ms`, five type sizes inside 480×200, and a right column that is specified rather than designed. `shape-rendering:crispEdges` plus a translating slider will snap-jitter per frame — a caliper that stutters is worse than one that bounces.

**3. Rules.** Gold breaks. In Bench, gold = "measured in production"; here it = "coincidence/precision." Two meanings, one section. CLAUDE.md §4 enumerates three sanctioned gold sites; coinciding vernier ticks is a fourth. Worse, "every visual is data" breaks: the jaws close on **200 ms, a limit, never a measured value**. The most prominent figure in the new visual is the one number that isn't evidence. `CERT No. 16b856c0` relabels a verifiable MD5 as institutional chrome — less checkable, not more.

**4. Collision.** Not Vitrine — that "caliper" is three lines. The collision is with **Bench itself**: already a `<figure>` with a `<figcaption>` that already assigns gold. You'd stack two captioned figures and push the evidence table ~260px further down a section whose source says "flat, dense and silent." And `<Caliper state="sourced">` sits centimetres from a literal illustrated caliper — the abstract mark demoted to a picture of itself.

**5. Buildable**, with one false premise: `--motion-ease-emphasized` is `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` — it does not overshoot. The "non-negotiable" new token is unjustified.

**Single strongest fix:** Invert it. Draw the **tag** as the object — clipped corner, punched eyelet, a hairline wire looping through it — and reduce the instrument to the cropped 150–250 ms window (the mobile viewBox, at every size), so only the jaws, the vernier and the limit bracket survive. Recognition then arrives from silhouette instead of from reading, the 2px coincidence becomes legible, four type sizes vanish, and the caliper stops competing with the Caliper mark. Close the jaws on nothing and print `TL 200 ms` alone — measure the limit, not an invented reading.
