## Why it still fails, stated without softening

The 1500 ms budget is consumed upstream and off-host, by two terms this repository does not own.

1. **The funded rung's own time.** `openai / gpt-4.1-mini` — the ladder's last rung, because the
   three above it are out of credit — took **790–2219 ms** across the ten runs. On `c3b` it took
   **2219 ms**: over the whole bar before one byte of transport. On `c4b` it took 1505 ms — also
   over the bar by itself. No free change moves this number; it is one call to one account.
2. **Transport, on a host at load ~13.** The Hosting route pays the origin's *completion* time as
   its first byte (Fastly buffers the SSE body — confirmed again here: all four Hosting cold runs
   delivered 48 delta frames inside 1–2 ms). `c4a` = 1831 ms of provider plus ~1470 ms of edge and
   client scheduling. The cold samples degrade monotonically across the window (1193 → 2522 → 2282
   → 3304 on Hosting) while the provider term moves far less, which points at the host, not the
   function. This is a stated confound, not a correction: a visitor on an idle machine pays less.

**Warm sends clear the bar comfortably** — 979 ms (origin) and 1080 ms (hosting), improved from the
review's 818/1320 ms on a busier host — and the shipped client fires `?warm=1` when the panel opens
(`lib/miniVicBrain.ts warmMiniVicChat`), so this is the path a visitor who opens the panel and types
actually takes. The failing path is the send that arrives with no panel-open ping ahead of it.

## What was NOT done, and why

- **The ladder was not inverted to `openai` first.** `docs/prompt.md` §0.4 fixes the order and is
  not latency-conditional; a previous revision that hardcoded `openai` first was already reverted
  for that reason. It would also buy nothing: the skip is already 0 ms on every sample above.
  `CHAT_PROVIDER_ORDER` remains the Owner's data-driven override.
- **No paid setting was enabled.** `minInstances` is untouched at its pre-existing value of **1**
  — it was already enabled before this task, so "buy a warm instance" is not an available saving.
  No provider account was topped up. ElevenLabs and D-ID were not called.
- **No site build, no browser.** The rendered panel is not measured here; the numbers are wire
  numbers from `node` `fetch`.

The residual is a cost decision and is written up as **OB-1** in `docs/delivery/OWNER-BLOCKED.md`.
